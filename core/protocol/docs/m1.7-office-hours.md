# M1.7 — Office Hours Cadence

**Decision: weekly, 30 minutes, Thursdays 16:00 UTC** — first meeting held
regardless of attendance, on or before two weeks after M1.3 (public repo) lands.
Zero-attendance is acceptable for the first 4-6 weeks; cadence persistence
matters more than attendance.

## Why office hours at this stage

The whitepaper (§7.2 M1.7, §A) and the project plan both make office hours a
**Phase 1 deliverable**, not a Phase 2 nice-to-have. The reason is asymmetric:

- **Cost of running** with zero attendees: negligible — 30 min on the calendar
- **Cost of NOT running** when external interest emerges (post M1.3 announcement,
  post TAG-AI submission, after a single KubeCon-floor mention): extreme,
  because there's no reliable channel for someone with feedback to reach a
  maintainer in real time, and async issue-tracker latency kills early
  community momentum

The CNCF Sandbox bar specifically values "regular, public, async-friendly
maintainer engagement." Empty office hours that are run consistently are a
stronger signal than scheduled-but-cancelled or scheduled-but-not-yet-started.

## Schedule

| | |
|---|---|
| **Day** | Thursday |
| **Time** | 16:00 UTC (12:00 EDT / 09:00 PDT / 17:00 BST / 18:00 CEST / 21:30 IST) |
| **Duration** | 30 minutes |
| **Cadence** | Weekly |
| **First meeting** | Within 2 weeks of M1.3 (public repo + governance scaffolding live) |
| **Format** | Open agenda; if no one shows, the maintainer publishes a 2-3 sentence "no agenda this week, here's what shipped" note |
| **Recording** | Yes by default, posted to the repo; participants can opt out by saying so before recording starts |

### Why Thursday 16:00 UTC

- **Globally tractable**: Mid-afternoon US East Coast, late afternoon Europe,
  late evening India. Excludes only US West Coast morning (9 AM PDT is fine
  for early risers) and Asia/Pacific late evening. No perfect time exists for
  a global audience; this minimizes total exclusion.
- **Mid-week**: Avoids Monday catch-up and Friday wind-down energy. Tuesday
  was considered but conflicts with several CNCF SIG meetings.
- **30 minutes**: long enough for one substantive discussion, short enough
  that empty meetings are easy to absorb. If a recurring 30-min block produces
  consistent overflow, switch to 45 or 60 min — but not before that signal exists.

## Mechanism

- **Hosting**: Zoom or Google Meet — whichever the eventual public org has
  set up. No platform decision before M1.3.
- **Agenda board**: GitHub Discussion category "office-hours-agenda" in the
  public repo. Anyone can post topics 24h before the meeting; maintainer
  triages. If no posts, maintainer drafts a "what shipped this week" note as
  the agenda.
- **Notes**: Posted to the same Discussion thread within 24h of the meeting,
  whether attendance was zero or substantial. Includes the recording link.
- **Reminder**: Calendar invite distributed via the project's mailing list
  (likely `opspact-dev@lists.cncf.io` if/when the project moves under CNCF;
  before that, a public Google Group).

## What "first meeting held regardless of attendance" looks like

Hypothetical first-meeting transcript posted to the agenda thread:

> Date: 2026-05-XX. Attendees: 1 (Gnani Rahul, maintainer). Notes: No agenda
> items submitted. Topic: status of v0.1 wire freeze (M1.6). Discussion: I
> walked through the protocol primitives and the open schema-trust question
> from §3.1 of the whitepaper. No live questions; happy to take async questions
> in this thread or via a new Discussion post.
>
> Recording: [link]
>
> Next meeting: same time, next Thursday.

This format communicates: the cadence is real, the maintainer is reachable,
the project is in motion, and there's a low-friction way to engage. It is
NOT theatre — actual zero-attendee meetings are a normal part of the early
project lifecycle and should be reported truthfully.

## Adjustments tied to community signal

- **First 4-6 weeks**: empty meetings expected; persist anyway.
- **Once 1-2 external attendees show up consistently**: continue same cadence;
  start using the 30 min for substantive design discussion if they have
  questions worth that depth.
- **Once 5+ external attendees regularly**: consider splitting into "design
  office hours" (developers / contributors) and "user office hours" (operators
  asking deployment questions). One slot continues at the original time;
  the other gets added at a complementary time zone.
- **If 12+ weeks pass with persistently zero attendees and no async engagement
  on issues**: stop the cadence; redirect that 30 min/week into recorded
  short-form videos answering hypothetical questions ("how does federation
  identity propagation work"). This is a community-signal failure, not an
  office-hours failure.

## Pre-M1.3 placeholder behavior

This decision is recorded now (2026-04-25) so M1.7 is not lost in the
M1.3-public-repo cutover. Until M1.3 lands:

- No Discussion category yet (no public repo)
